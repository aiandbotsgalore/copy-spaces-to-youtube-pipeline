# FORENSIC EVIDENTIARY GAP ANALYSIS & STRESS TEST
### Audit of Claims, Allegations, and Witness Testimony for *The Paper Trail*

**Governing Objective:** To prevent uncorroborated allegations from being presented as established facts, ensure zero defamatory exposure, and maintain absolute journalistic rigor.

For every major accusation or factual claim in the documentary, this audit evaluates eight mandatory verification criteria:
1. **Exact Statement:** What exactly did the speaker say on the record?
2. **Claimed Evidence:** What evidence did the speaker claim supported the assertion?
3. **Primary Evidence in Possession:** What primary documentation or audio do we actually possess?
4. **Evidentiary Support:** Does the available evidence support the accusation?
5. **Partial Support / Nuance:** Does it support only part of the claim?
6. **Contradicting Primary Sources:** Does another primary witness or document dispute the interpretation?
7. **Resolution Status:** Is the claim verified, partially verified, uncorroborated, disputed, or impossible to determine?
8. **Contextual Omissions:** Was anything material omitted from the public presentation?

## 1. The "Predator" & Interpersonal Abuse Allegation

- **1. Exact Statement:** (`AQ-0001`, `AQ-0002`) Angela claimed: *"I called you a predator because that is what you are. The behavior is predatory. You seek out impressionable girls with trauma issues, and then you triangulate them against each other... I called you out for making my friend suicidal."*
- **2. Claimed Evidence:** Angela claimed to possess extensive screenshots of text messages between Logan and Sav demonstrating emotional abuse and manipulation.
- **3. Primary Evidence in Possession:** Verbatim audio from *LOGAN BLACK UNMASKED* (`20260918_1MJgNbdaAPlGL`), audio of *Angela_Maggard_fan_club* (`20260918_1AxRnZMeZNYxl`), and Sav's live microphone statement (`AQ-0213`).
- **4. Evidentiary Support:** **UNSUPPORTED AS A FACTUAL CHARGE.** The evidence establishes an acrimonious personal breakup between two consenting adults in which harsh words were exchanged during high emotional volatility. It does not establish criminal or predatory conduct.
- **5. Partial Support / Nuance:** The screenshots substantiate that Logan and Sav had emotionally heated arguments during their breakup.
- **6. Contradicting Primary Sources:** **SAV DIRECTLY REFUTES THE CLAIM.** In `AQ-0213`, Sav took the microphone live on audio to state: *"For, like, Logan, all this stuff that's happening to him. None of this should be happening to him because, you know, I spun out and was crazy... I have told Angela that she has, like, the wrong guys... these are the people that have really helped her."*
- **7. Resolution Status:** **DISPUTED / REFUTED BY PRIMARY SOURCE.** The alleged victim explicitly denied being victimized in the manner Angela claimed and condemned Angela's use of her personal life.
- **8. Contextual Omissions:** Angela omitted that Sav repeatedly begged her not to share the private messages and that Sav attributed the conflict to a standard romantic falling out exacerbated by backchannel gossip.
- **Editorial Rule for Documentary:** Present Angela's statement as an unverified allegation; follow immediately with Sav's live refutation. Logan's narration must explicitly state that Sav rejected the "predator" framing.

# THE PAPER TRAIL — revised audio narrative architecture
**Status:** Editorial proposal grounded in the current manuscript, Fable 5.2's review, the local master quote collection, and sampled primary transcripts. This is not an assertion that disputed events are proven.  
**Current manuscript:** \`MasterPapertrai.txt\` (10 numbered scenes, four acts, prologue and epilogue).  
**Creative principle:** The audience discovers the significance of recorded conduct through scenes, not a host reciting a prosecution case. Angela and the people around her must remain complicated humans.

## The central question

**What becomes of the private things people share in trust when friendship turns into investigation and public conflict?**

This naturally contains the particular allegation that Angela used vulnerability against people, but it does not predetermine every motive. A companion question: **when does documentation protect someone, and when can its later use harm them?**

## Editorial decisions embodied here

1. **Never treat chronology as a suspense trick.** The October group chat follows the September 18 statement. The Deacon source was imported under a later tag, but is demonstrably replayed in the Sept 18 Fan Club: confirm its original recording date before a day-specific narration claim.
2. **Don't assert a single motive.** One person can be frightened, angry, convinced of wrongdoing, and vindictive in the same conflict. Cite what people said and did, not an invented private mental state.
3. **Keep meaningful discomfort for Logan.** His AI-image account and supportive exchanges with Angela belong in the story if significant; do not remove them because they complicate an adversarial argument.
4. **Preserve Sav's agency.** Sav reports her own involvement in backchannel conflict and accepts responsibility for alarming statements; that makes her first-person plea and the subsequent handling of private material more consequential.
5. **Save the most powerful archival tape for real scenes.** Do not chop a long confrontation into phrases and narrate over it. The Deacon confrontation and Sav's uninterrupted Sept 18 account are principal scenes.
6. **Audio ethics:** A synthetic performance of a text message must be disclosed as a reading, not mistaken for a recovered phone call or authentic recorded voice. Prefer Logan or a neutral reader for unverified texts. Never broadcast a crisis recording as a dramatic payoff.
7. **No mechanical runtime or silence quota.** Retain listening space when justified; do not add or remove a fixed number of pauses.

# CONTRADICTION & CALLBACK MATRIX
### Master Analytical Juxtaposition for *The Paper Trail*

This document provides a systematic, side-by-side evidentiary pairing of Angela Maggard's statements across time. It matches her stated standards, ethical rules, and interpersonal commitments **BEFORE** the fallout (February 2026) directly against her statements, tactics, and conduct **AFTER** the fallout (September 2026).

**Editorial Rule:** The documentary does not tell the listener "look at this hypocrisy." The edit presents the earlier statement, presents the later statement, and allows the contrast to land with silence.

## 2. Why the Previous Architecture Was Flawed: The Strategic Reset

The initial production proposal suffered from critical architectural flaws:
1. **Narration-Heavy Bias:** Subordinated Angela's primary audio to host exposition. The new model strictly reverses this: **IF ANGELA CAN SAY IT HERSELF, LOGAN GENERALLY DOES NOT SAY IT FOR HER.**
2. **Artificial Structural Constraints:** Imposed an arbitrary 6-act structure, fixed 46:30 duration, and 32-clip cap before the evidentiary story was fully mapped.
3. **Premature Scripting:** Wrote narration copy before mapping verbatim contradiction pairings and conducting an evidence stress test.
4. **Melodramatic Tropes:** Relied on loaded chapter titles, trailer-style sound effects, and constant bass drones rather than letting verbatim audio land dry with silence.

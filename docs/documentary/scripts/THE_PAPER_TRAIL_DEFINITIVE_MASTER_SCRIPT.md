## Production & Acoustic Conventions
- **Voiceover Delivery:** Intimate, grounded, observant, conversational, measured, and authentic. No true-crime trailer melodrama or performative outrage.
- **Audio Texture:** Clean archival audio, voice-forward, dry acoustic spaces.
- **Scoring & Ambience:** Subtle, low acoustic bass pulses and restrained room-tone beds. Music never competes with dialogue. No cinematic risers, gunshots, explosions, or fake sound effects.
- **The Role of Silence:** Silence is an active instrument of storytelling. When a devastating archival statement lands, allow 2 to 3 full seconds of room tone or complete silence before narration resumes.
- **Evidence Labeling:** Every archival passage is attributed with its verified catalog Quote ID (`AQ-xxxx`), speaker attribution, and master audio file location.

## 1. Governing Objective & Core Mandate

This production is an independent, standalone investigative audio documentary reconstructing the recorded statements, evidentiary methodologies, interpersonal alliances, and subsequent public campaigns of Angela Maggard across Twitter Spaces between February and October 2026.

### 1.1 No Predetermined Runtime
- The documentary has **no predetermined runtime**.
- It must be exactly as long as necessary to tell the story properly while maintaining high listenability, evidentiary rigor, pacing, entertainment value, and narrative engagement.
- Preferred upper limit: under approximately one hour (~60 minutes).
- Realistic possibilities: 25 minutes, 38 minutes, 48 minutes, 55 minutes, etc. The final duration must **emerge organically from the finished story**, not from a pre-allocated broadcast clock.

### 1.2 No Clip Quota
- There is **no predetermined number of audio clips**.
- The former limit of 32 clips is permanently abolished.
- The project draws from a master corpus of 283 verified quotes across 35 recorded episodes. Every statement that materially advances the narrative, substantiates a factual point, or illustrates a behavioral pattern will be included. Every statement that introduces redundant padding or listener fatigue will be excised.

## 2. Most Important Editorial Principle: The Quote-First Inversion

### 2.1 Angela as Principal Narrator
The traditional true-crime model—where a host provides continuous narrative exposition and inserts short audio soundbites to illustrate host assertions—is **strictly reversed**.

> **THE GOVERNING RULE:**  
> **IF ANGELA CAN SAY IT HERSELF, LOGAN SHOULD GENERALLY NOT SAY IT FOR HER.**

Through her voluminous recorded history on Twitter Spaces, Angela Maggard has documented her own biography, investigative philosophies, data collection practices, interpersonal alliances, personal grievances, tactical maneuvers, and post-fallout justifications.

Angela must effectively serve as one of the principal narrators of the documentary through her archived recordings. Sequences will regularly feature Angela speaking uninterrupted for 30, 60, or 90+ seconds across carefully edited archival clips, with minimal or zero host interjection.

### 2.2 The Ten Functions of Logan's Narration
Logan Black's spoken narration is strictly confined to ten defined editorial functions:
1. **Establish chronology:** Ground the listener in calendar dates, temporal lapses, and sequence of events.
2. **Provide indispensable context:** Supply necessary background that audio recordings alone cannot convey (e.g., off-platform circumstances, platform mechanics).
3. **Identify source, date, and venue:** Clearly attribute the specific Twitter Space title, host, and recording date.
4. **Connect scenes:** Bridge disparate thematic blocks smoothly without rhetorical filler.
5. **Distinguish claim from established fact:** Explicitly separate unverified assertions from proven reality.
6. **Explain what underlying evidence actually establishes:** Clarify the legal and factual boundaries of documents, screenshots, and police reports.
7. **Identify missing evidence:** Highlight instances where an explosive allegation was made but no supporting documentation was ever produced.
8. **Introduce contradictions:** Juxtapose earlier statements with later conduct, allowing the contrast to register naturally.
9. **Prevent misleading edits:** Ensure surrounding context is preserved so that no party's words are distorted or taken out of context.
10. **Clarify what remains disputed or unknown:** Transparently communicate ambiguities where the factual record is unresolved.

## 3. Core Documentary Questions

The entire presentation is organized around two core investigative inquiries:

1. **The Standard of Documentation:**  
   *"What happens when Angela Maggard's own standards of evidence, documentation, timelines, patterns, transparency, and accountability are applied to Angela Maggard?"*

2. **The Anatomy of the Reversal:**  
   *"How did someone who describes herself as Logan's close friend—who publicly defended him against severe rumors and entrusted him with her contingency plans—come to publicly brand him a predatory monster and initiate an escalating scorched-earth campaign against him?"*

These questions replace simplistic accusations (such as "Angela lies"). The documentary reconstructs the historical record and allows the listener to observe where statements are:
- Fully supported,
- Partially supported,
- Contradicted by her own prior words,
- Contradicted by primary witnesses,
- Misleadingly framed,
- Exaggerated,
- Unsupported by any available evidence,
- Or fundamentally impossible to determine.

## 5. Strict Standards of Language & Ethics

To maintain unassailable journalistic integrity and legal compliance, the following editorial guardrails are absolute:

### 5.1 Prohibited Editorial Behaviors
- **NO Psychological Diagnoses:** Never label Angela (or any subject) as "narcissistic," "sociopathic," "borderline," "histrionic," or "mentally ill."
- **NO Unsubstantiated Criminal Allegations:** Never assert that an individual committed a crime unless there is an official conviction, formal indictment, or verified public record. All claims remain "allegations" or "statements."
- **NO Melodramatic True-Crime Tropes:** Ban cliché transition phrases ("Little did he know...", "What happened next would shock everyone...", "A web of deceit...").
- **NO Biased Chapter Titles:** Loaded labels (e.g., "Weaponized Welfare Check", "Manufactured Smears", "Forensic Verdict") are prohibited. Scene headings must be descriptive, chronological, and thematic (e.g., "The Contingency Custodian", "The Welfare Check Sequence", "The Discovery Strategy").
- **NO False Triumphalism:** Logan does not deliver a self-congratulatory monologue at the conclusion. The documentary ends with the evidence and Angela's own summary words.

### 5.2 Mandatory Evidentiary Tagging
Every statement, document, and audio cue must fall into one of seven clear evidentiary classifications:
1. `[FACT]`: Indisputable, document-backed historical event (e.g., Space broadcast dates, timestamps, verified DM logs).
2. `[ANGELA CLAIM]`: A statement made on mic or in text by Angela Maggard.
3. `[LOGAN CLAIM]`: A statement made on mic or in text by Logan Black.
4. `[SAV CLAIM]`: A statement made on mic or in text by Sav.
5. `[WITNESS TESTIMONY]`: Firsthand oral accounts provided by third parties (Sian, Timmy, Glow Baby, Orr, Cambian). Must be identified as subjective witness testimony.
6. `[INFERENCE]`: A logical deduction drawn directly from established facts, explicitly labeled as an inference.
7. `[UNKNOWN]`: Unresolved matters where evidence is contradictory, missing, or permanently private.

## 6. The Narrative Spine

The presentation follows an organic ten-movement progression:

$$\text{TRUST} \longrightarrow \text{METHOD} \longrightarrow \text{ETHICAL RULES} \longrightarrow \text{SAV} \longrightarrow \text{REVERSAL} \longrightarrow \text{ARCHIVE} \longrightarrow \text{PUBLIC ACCUSATION} \longrightarrow \text{ESCALATION} \longrightarrow \text{CONTRADICTION} \longrightarrow \text{PAPER TRAIL}$$

1. **PROLOGUE (Angela Defines the Rules):** Angela's own audio sets the evidentiary contract. Logan accepts the terms in one word.
2. **TRUST (The Pre-Fallout Alliance):** Early 2026. Mutual defense, co-hosting, Logan as confidant and legal contingency custodian.
3. **METHOD (The Operating System):** Neutral examination of Angela's methods: AWDTSM groups, Discord infiltration, target lists, civil discovery baiting, data retention.
4. **ETHICAL RULES (Standards Before the War):** Angela's pre-fallout rules: decrying "male witch hunts", defending against guilt by association, insisting evidence must serve a purpose rather than "twist the knife."
5. **SAV (The Interpersonal Shift):** Sav enters Logan's life and Angela's life. Angela transitions from friend to evaluator/investigator.
6. **REVERSAL (The Trigger):** The breakup of Logan and Sav. The welfare check address relay. Angela's stated catalyst: *"And I got triggered. And so I sent the screenshot proving he was a liar. And that's when shit hit the fan."* (`AQ-0270`).
7. **ARCHIVE (The Vault Opens):** Private Discord dossiers, withholding evidence, provocation, and the transition into the *"fuck it bucket"* (`AQ-0016`).
8. **PUBLIC ACCUSATION (The Case Against Logan):** Angela's allegations presented thoroughly and fairly: abuse, triangulation, revenge porn, predatory behavior. Sav's live microphone appearance disputing Angela's actions and exonerating Logan.
9. **ESCALATION (Real-World Machinery):** The conflict leaves the digital realm: process servers, subpoenas, police/FBI reports, CPS calls, parole officers, infant child doxxing. Angela's admissions: *"Process used as a club, both sides, myself included"* (`AQ-0022`), *"I identify as Tannerite"* (`AQ-0276`).
10. **CONTRADICTION & THE PAPER TRAIL:** Systematic confrontation of Angela's early ethical principles with her subsequent actions. Angela receives the final word.

## 7. Acoustic & Technical Philosophy

- **Intelligibility Over Spectacle:** The human voice carries 100% of the documentary's power.
- **Dynamic Silence:** Pauses and hard cuts to silence after significant admissions provide evidentiary weight. Do not plaster emotional music over revelations.
- **Fair Acoustic Framing:** Historical clips are normalized for vocal clarity and intelligibility without synthetic filtering that sounds cartoonish or distortive.
- **Engineering Precision:** Integrated program loudness must hit standard stereo distribution targets (-16.0 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP ceiling). Music ducking is adjusted dynamically scene by scene, never by rigid automated sidechain formulas.
